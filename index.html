    Gods in Color

    The color reconstructions shown here come from Gods in Color (Bunte Götter), the polychromy research project led for more than four decades by archaeologists Vinzenz Brinkmann and Ulrike Koch-Brinkmann at the Liebieghaus Skulpturensammlung, Frankfurt am Main.

    Their full-size reconstructions, based on scientific study of pigment traces and paint ghosts, have toured internationally since 2003. The expanded exhibition Gods in Color: Golden Edition ran at the Liebieghaus from January 2020 to September 2021.
